HSBC PRODUCTO BASICO GENERAL - COMPLIMENTARY INSURANCE COVERAGE

Coverage 1. Fraud protection. The account holder is covered at no cost against unauthorized debit card transactions reported within the period defined in the policy, up to the insured amount stated in the certificate.

Coverage 2. Life insurance. A complimentary life insurance benefit is paid to the designated beneficiaries upon the death of the account holder, subject to the exclusions listed in the policy.

Exclusions. Coverage does not apply to transactions authorized with the customer's PIN or one-time password, nor to losses reported after the reporting window has expired.
