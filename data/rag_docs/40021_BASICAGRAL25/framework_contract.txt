HSBC - GENERAL TERMS AND CONDITIONS FOR DEPOSIT ACCOUNTS

Article 1. Applicability. These terms govern all HSBC demand deposit accounts, including basic, payroll and premium accounts, together with the fee schedule of each product.

Article 2. Insufficient funds. A cheque returned because of insufficient funds generates a fee per event as published in the product fee schedule.

Article 3. Unjustified disputes. If the customer files a charge dispute that, after investigation, is found to be unjustified, the Bank may charge an invalid-claim fee per event.

Article 4. Statements. Account statements are made available monthly through online banking. Paper statements can be requested at a branch.

Article 5. Deposit insurance. Deposits are protected by IPAB up to 400,000 UDIs per person per institution.
