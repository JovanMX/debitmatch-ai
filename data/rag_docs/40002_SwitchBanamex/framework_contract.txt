BANAMEX - FRAMEWORK CONTRACT FOR DEMAND DEPOSIT ACCOUNTS

Clause 1. Scope. This contract applies to all Banamex debit and payroll accounts. Each product has its own fee schedule annex.

Clause 2. Digital opening. Accounts opened through the mobile app require identity validation with an official ID and biometric verification. Account limits may apply until the customer completes identification at a branch.

Clause 3. Transfers. SPEI and same-bank transfers through digital channels are executed in real time during SPEI operating hours.

Clause 4. Deposit insurance. Deposits are protected by IPAB up to 400,000 UDIs per person per institution.
