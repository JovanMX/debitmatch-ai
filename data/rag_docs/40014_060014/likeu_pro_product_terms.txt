SANTANDER DEBITO LIKEU PRO - PRODUCT-SPECIFIC TERMS

Section 1. Minimum average balance. The customer must maintain a minimum monthly average balance of MXN 3,000.00. If the monthly average balance falls below that amount, the Bank will charge a below-minimum-balance fee of MXN 140.00 for that month. The fee is waived for any month in which the average balance is equal to or higher than MXN 3,000.00.

Section 2. Payroll deposits. The account accepts payroll direct deposits from any employer. Payroll benefits apply only while at least one payroll deposit is received per month.

Section 3. Digital channels. Same-bank transfers through the mobile banking app or online banking are free of charge. Interbank SPEI transfers requested at a branch cost MXN 100.00 per transaction and CECOBAN transfers at a branch cost MXN 35.00 per transaction.

Section 4. Inactivity. An account inactivity fee of MXN 50.00 per event applies according to the general framework contract.
