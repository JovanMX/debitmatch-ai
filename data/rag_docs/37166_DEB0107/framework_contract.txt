BANCO DEL BIENESTAR - GENERAL TERMS FOR BASIC DEPOSIT ACCOUNTS

Clause 1. Scope. These terms apply to the Debicuenta Basica and to welfare payroll accounts of Banco del Bienestar.

Clause 2. No maintenance fee. Basic accounts do not charge a monthly maintenance fee and do not require a minimum balance.

Clause 3. Branch operations. Deposits, withdrawals and transfers are performed at branches and teller windows. Interbank SPEI transfers at a branch are free of charge.

Clause 4. Balance limits. Basic accounts are subject to the monthly deposit limits established by Banco de Mexico for low-risk accounts.
